About Me

My name is Elon Reeve Musk. I was born in South Africa in 1971, I am a South African-born Canadian-American entrepreneur, business magnate, industrial designer, and engineer. I am best known for co-founding Tesla Motors and SpaceX, but my journey to wealth began with co-founding PayPal. I also founded Zip2, a company that provided online newspapers with maps and business directories. I am also known for his involvement in OpenAI and Neuralink.
